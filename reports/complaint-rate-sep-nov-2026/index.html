Method. Every archived LiveChat chat in the period was pulled per platform. Customers' messages were screened for complaint language in all supported languages (Tagalog, Bisaya, English, Bengali, Hindi, Vietnamese, Portuguese, Spanish, Malay, Burmese), and each flagged transcript (plus every negative rating) was read and classified by AI into the complaint types above. A chat counts once even if the customer complained several times. Complaints about game results, deposits or bonuses are not counted as CS complaints unless the customer blames how the agent or bot handled it.

Out of scope: WellyTalk platforms (MMKMAX, MM01/MM03/MM05/MM07) as requested. Platforms with no LiveChat group: NG05 (DEY77-NG), PH37 (199JILI). Groups 30 and 96 are shared by two platform codes, so their chats are split evenly between them.
